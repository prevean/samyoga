About Preethi

Welcome! I'm Preethi, a certified yoga teacher passionate about helping students build strength, flexibility, balance, and inner calm through yoga.

My yoga journey began in 2008 when I started practicing to relieve back pain. As I continued, I experienced improvements not only in my physical health but also in my overall well-being, energy, and mood. Yoga gradually became much more than a form of exercise—it became a way of living with greater awareness and balance.

Inspired by these life-changing benefits, I wanted to understand yoga more deeply: how it works, why it works, and the philosophy behind the practice. This curiosity led me to complete both my 200-hour and 300-hour Yoga Teacher Training certifications.

These experiences gave me a deeper understanding of yoga's physical, mental, and emotional benefits while also giving greater meaning and purpose to my own journey. Today, I love sharing that knowledge with others by creating a welcoming and supportive space where students of all levels can build strength, improve flexibility, reduce stress, and develop a lasting connection with themselves through yoga.
