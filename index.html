About Me

Welcome! I'm Preethi, a certified yoga teacher passionate about helping students build strength, flexibility, balance, and inner calm through yoga. I began doing yoga in 2008 to help with my back pain. Soon I realized and reaped many benefits from getting better in general health and mood. I wanted to go deep into yoga and how it works and why it works. Hence I did my teacher training in 2021, completing both 200hr and 300hr courses gave me not only great insights about the benefits but also meaning and purpose for my yoga journey.
